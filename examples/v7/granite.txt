Instrumental alternative rock trio, live drum kit, electric bass and interlocking electric guitars. A tense clean offset-guitar hook in a small room grows into broken grunge backbeats and heavy syncopated open-string downstrokes. Tuneful picked guitar against the low riff, bass moves through the spaces in the kick. A quiet suspended turn, a developed fuzz lead with bent notes and breathing phrases, then a full-band return and a ringing natural ending. Wide shoegaze feedback only at peaks, warm live studio dynamics. All melody is played on instruments.
